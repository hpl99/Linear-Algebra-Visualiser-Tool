About this lab

Linear Algebra Lab is a simulation-first playground. Every experiment shows the same idea three ways: a 3D picture, live numbers, and the formula behind it.

  • Drag the glowing spheres to move vectors; orbit with the mouse, scroll to zoom.
  • Matrix experiments warp a second grid. Scrub Transformation Flow from Identity to M.
  • Shortcuts: Ctrl+K / “/” search, E explorer, G grid, M music, F focus, I info, R reset camera, Space play flow.

A NumPy companion for all 17 experiments is in linear_algebra_lab.py.
